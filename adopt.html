The process

Adopting a cat from us is straightforward. You will fill out an application, meet the cat, and if it is a good match we will guide you through the paperwork.

  1. Complete the adoption application

    If you are unsure which cat you wish to adopt, just fill in “any cat/kitten” in the field for Cat’s Name.

  2. We review it and reach out

    Our team will review your application and contact you to discuss the next steps.

  3. Meet the cat in person

    We always encourage potential adopters to visit our Saturday adoption event at PetSmart in Clearwater, or our Cat Haven for older cats, and meet the cats in person.

  4. Paperwork and home

    We aim to ensure that both you and the cat are ready for a loving home together.

Why adopt?

Adopting a pet means giving a deserving animal a second chance at life. You will gain a loyal companion while reducing the number of homeless pets in our community.

Benefits of adoption

When you adopt, you are not just saving a life. You will enjoy a friend for life, improve your mental health, and make a positive impact on our local shelters and the environment.

Join our mission

Supporting pet adoption helps us create a caring community. Whether through adopting, fostering, or volunteering, your involvement makes a real difference.

The fee, and what it covers

The present adoption fee is $150.00. Please note this amount may change without notification.

Every adoptable cat and kitten comes to you already:

  • Spayed or neutered
  • Microchipped, including the registration fee
  • Dewormed
  • Treated for fleas
  • Given age-appropriate vaccinations

Our overall expenditures last year put our cost per adoption at $146, so the fee very nearly covers the cat in front of you and no more.

What we ask of you

Before adoption, we will ask you to provide:

  • Your address, and home and work telephone numbers
  • Your driver’s license number and a photo ID, a copy of which is attached to the contract at the time of adoption
  • Proof that pets are allowed, if you rent

You must agree to keep the cat inside. This applies to every cat we place.

Questions we get asked

What is the adoption process?

Adopting a pet from us is straightforward. You will fill out an application, meet the pet, and if you have a good match, we will guide you through the paperwork. We aim to ensure that both you and the pet are ready for a loving home together.

What are the adoption fees?

The present adoption fee is $150.00, and it may change without notification. It helps cover medical care, food and shelter for our cats. Our goal is to make adoption affordable while ensuring each cat receives quality care before going to their forever home.

Can I meet the cats before adopting?

Yes, and we encourage it. This is crucial to ensure that both you and the animal feel comfortable and connected before making a lifelong commitment to one another. Visit our Saturday adoption event at PetSmart Countryside, or contact us to arrange an appointment at Cat Haven for older cats.

What if I have pets at home?

If you have other pets, we recommend a meet-and-greet before adoption. We want to make sure that your new cat will fit well within your family, and proper introductions help everyone adjust comfortably.

What if I want to return my pet?

We understand that circumstances can change. If you ever need to return your cat, please contact us at sos@saveourstraysinc.com or call 727-481-5262. Our priority is to ensure the well-being of the animal, and we are here to help you through that process.

How can I support Save Our Strays?

Adopt, foster, volunteer, or donate. Fostering and volunteering and donating both have their own pages.

Have more questions?

We are here to assist you. There is no wrong question, and no obligation.
