Where the money goes $146

Our overall expenditures for the past year put our cost per adoption at $146. The $150 adoption fee very nearly covers the cat in front of you, and no more. Everything else we do runs on donations.

63%

Veterinary care, our largest expenditure, from routine spays and neuters to major surgeries for amputations, broken bones, eye problems and internal ailments.

20%

Cat food and litter, nearly a fifth of everything we spend.

12%

Administrative costs, including two dedicated phone lines, storage rental, printing of adoption contracts, newsletters and brochures, and stamps.

$15

Per microchip, including the registration fee. Every SOS cat is microchipped for identification.

The $146 cost per adoption does not factor in our long-term care for unadoptable felines, which is part of our no-kill policy. Those cats stay with us for life, and donations are what make that possible.
