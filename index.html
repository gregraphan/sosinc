Who we are

Incorporated in 1994, for one purpose

Save Our Strays was incorporated in the fall of 1994 with a charitable purpose: to rescue stray cats and kittens in Pinellas County, Florida, provide medical evaluation and treatment if necessary, spay or neuter each animal, place these cats and kittens in temporary shelter homes or facilities pending adoption, and find them good permanent homes.

We are a non-profit organization with a 501(c)(3) designation. You may reach us at 727-481-5262.

See our paperwork and our finances

Where the money goes $146

Our overall expenditures last year put our cost per adoption at $146. The $150 adoption fee very nearly covers the cat in front of you, and no more.

63%

Veterinary care, our largest expenditure, from routine spays and neuters to major surgeries for amputations, broken bones, eye problems and internal ailments.

20%

Cat food and litter, nearly a fifth of everything we spend.

12%

Administrative costs, including two dedicated phone lines, storage rental and printing.

$15

Per microchip, including the registration fee. Every SOS cat is microchipped.

Why adopt?

Adopting a cat means giving a deserving animal a second chance at life. You will gain a loyal companion while reducing the number of homeless pets in our community.

A lifelong companion

Adopting a pet means having a loyal friend by your side. Pets provide companionship and can brighten even the toughest days.

Improved mental health

Studies show that pets can help reduce stress and anxiety. The unconditional love they give promotes emotional well-being.

Unconditional love

Pets provide loyalty and love without conditions. They have a unique way of making you feel appreciated and valued.

An active lifestyle

Owning a pet encourages you to stay active. Walks, if you can keep your cat on a leash, and playtime keep both you and your pet healthy and engaged.

Life lessons

Through pet ownership, you learn responsibility, empathy, and how to care for another being, enriching your character.

Community connection

Adopting a pet connects you to a community of fellow animal lovers. It fosters friendships and support within neighborhoods.

Common questions

How can I adopt a cat?

Start by filling out our online adoption application. Once submitted, our team will review it and reach out to you to discuss the next steps. We always encourage potential adopters to visit our Saturday adoption event at PetSmart in Clearwater, or our Cat Haven for older cats, and meet the cats in person.

Read the full adoption process

What is the adoption fee?

The present adoption fee is $150.00, and it helps cover medical care, food and shelter for our cats. Please note this amount may change without notification. All of our adoptable kitties have been spayed or neutered, microchipped, dewormed, treated for fleas, and have received age-appropriate vaccinations.

Can I foster a cat?

Yes. Fostering means taking a cat or kitten home and caring for them until they are ready for adoption. Save Our Strays provides food and litter if requested, a cage or condo cage if necessary, and covers medical expenses.

See what fostering involves

How can I volunteer?

Complete our volunteer application. Once we receive it, we can discuss available roles and schedule an orientation to help you get started. Generally, volunteers must be at least 18 years of age, but there may be opportunities for those younger than 18 to help with fundraising.

What are my donation options?

You can support us through a one-time donation or set up a recurring monthly donation, by credit card or PayPal. You can also shop our wish lists on Chewy and Amazon. All contributions go directly to providing care and resources for our cats.

See all the ways to give

How can I help promote adoption?

Spread the word. Share our social media posts, tell friends about our programs, or even host an adoption event. Every conversation helps connect our cats with people looking to adopt.
