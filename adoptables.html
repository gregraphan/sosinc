No cats match those filters right now

That does not mean we cannot help. Our cats move between foster homes, Cat Haven and PetSmart, and additional kittens are often at PetSmart before they reach this page.

Apply anyway and tell us what you are looking for

Photographs only tell you so much

This is Tommy, being a kitten. A still photo of a cat asleep on a blanket tells you very little about what they are like awake.

That is why we encourage you to meet a cat in person before adopting, at PetSmart on a Saturday or by appointment at Cat Haven.

Where to meet them

Two things to know before you contact us

Cats come in and out of foster homes, so seeing a cat here does not guarantee they will still be available when you contact us. We also do not adopt on a first come, first served basis.

An application is required to move forward with any adoption, and a contract is completed after approval by one of our adoption volunteers. Renters will be asked to provide proof that pets are allowed. No phone calls, please.

Start an adoption application

More kittens than you see here

Additional kittens may be available at PetSmart before they are entered into the system. Come out to PetSmart Countryside on a Saturday between 10:30 AM and 2:30 PM to see the latest batch of kittens.

Both adoption locations and hours

Not sure which cat is right for you?

You do not have to decide before you apply. If you are unsure which cat you wish to adopt, just fill in “any cat/kitten” in the field for Cat’s Name, and we will talk it through with you.

Start an adoption application

Where our cats live

We are not a single building. Save Our Strays has an adoption facility available by appointment only, and also uses foster homes and space donated by local pet stores.

The cat you are interested in may be at Cat Haven in Pinellas Park, in a volunteer’s foster home, or at PetSmart Countryside on a Saturday. When you contact us for an appointment, please let us know which cat you wish to see and we will tell you where they are.
